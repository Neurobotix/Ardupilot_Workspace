# Audits

Audits, incidents, errata, and investigation records belong here. These records
explain what was found, when it was found, and what current docs or evidence
supersede.

## Naming

- New audit, incident, and errata records use
  `YYYY-MM-DD_lower_snake_case.md`.
- Examples: `2026-05-24_naming_policy_review.md`,
  `2026-05-24_runtime_path_incident.md`.
- Date prefixes are required for new audit records because they are historical
  events.
- Imported raw audit packages and older records may keep their original names
  when renaming would weaken provenance or require broad reference updates.

Do not put ADRs or evidence reports here. ADRs belong in
`governance/decisions/`; evidence reports belong in `evidence/reports/`.
