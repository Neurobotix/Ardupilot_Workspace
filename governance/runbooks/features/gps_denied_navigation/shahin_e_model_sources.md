Change from the earlier plan: knob 2 is now motor thrust, not drag. Motor and propeller are both
unknown, so thrust is the honest free value. Drag stays tied to a physical assumption (L/D) that the
power-off glide in the next calibration flight checks independently. When the engineers send the
motor (kV) and battery, knob 2 can be cross-checked: full speed in rad/s ~ kV x volts x 0.105.

## 5. Parameters

`config/vehicles/shahin_e.parm` = the 1104 log params minus 569 hardware-specific ones (compass
and IMU calibration, sensor IDs, ports, OSD, battery wiring, fence, board trim; the full list is at
the end of the file), plus a short SITL section (JSON backend, simulated airspeed, calm SITL wind,
SITL battery). All flight, navigation, TECS, limits, takeoff, landing and servo params are unchanged,
except one documented SITL exception: `EK3_SRC3_POSZ` 2 -> 1. Source set 3 is a backup selected only by
an RC switch; its height source is the real aircraft's rangefinder, which SITL does not simulate (see
change log, 2026-10-15). Source set 1, used in flight, is unchanged. The real flap input
(`RC9_OPTION 208`) and battery capacity (`BATT_CAPACITY 24000`) are kept.

Other observations: COMPASS_MOTCT is 0 (no motor-current compensation configured on the real aircraft),
EK3_IMU_MASK is 1 (one IMU used), LOG_REPLAY is 1 but the log has no EKF3 messages.

## 8. Open questions

- Why the windy window needs about +4.5 % throttle and +1 deg pitch: wind, altitude, gentle climbs, or battery
  voltage. Test later in simulation by adding wind alone and comparing.
- Real CG position (ruler measurement), tail dimensions, motor kV and propeller, takeoff method.

### Known limitations (reviewer decision 2026-10-18)

- **Full-throttle thrust is not validated.** Gate 2 takeoff: 1.9 -> 19.3 m/s in 1.6 s (~11 m/s^2),
  static thrust about equal to weight. KNOB 2 calibrates cruise thrust only. Likely cause: the
  copied Mini Talon blade model gives a very steep thrust-vs-rpm curve.
- **Pitch response at high speed / full throttle is not validated.** Gate 2: pitch rose to 53 deg
  after rotation with the elevator at 87 % of down travel, then a pitch oscillation that settled in
  about 20 s. Likely the same cause (excess thrust and speed). In level flight at 17 m/s the elevator
  sits 4.6 % from trim.
- Both are revisited when the engineers send the motor and propeller. If the propeller model
  changes, Gate 3 is rerun. Gate 4 (climb at ~58 % throttle) shows whether this matters near cruise.
