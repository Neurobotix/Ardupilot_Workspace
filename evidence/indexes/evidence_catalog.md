# Evidence Catalog

Date: 2026-05-25

Scope: cross-phase catalog for reviewed operational proof. The Phase 4 asset
and parameter/config indexes remain specialized indexes:
`evidence/indexes/asset_index.md` and
`evidence/indexes/parameter_config_index.md`.

Status values used here:

- `reference`: retained context or historical proof;
- `verified`: reviewed proof supporting the named claim;
- `superseded`: retained proof replaced by a later record;
- `incident`: proof tied to an audit or failure analysis;
- `blocked`: reviewed work that cannot support the intended claim yet.
